Diet Coke Bot – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Diet Coke Bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users and Discord servers interact with it.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled by a server, the Bot may process or store:

Discord Identifiers

- Discord user IDs.
- Discord server (guild) IDs.
- Channel IDs and message IDs.
- Role IDs and other configuration identifiers.

User-Related Information

- Economy balances and activity statistics.
- Level and experience information.
- AFK status and messages.
- Reminder text and scheduled reminder information.
- Poll votes.
- Moderation warnings and related records.
- User-related moderation and role-management records.

Server Configuration

- Custom command prefixes.
- Welcome messages and channel settings.
- Moderation and security settings.
- Ticket configurations.
- Autoresponder triggers and responses.
- Custom tags, polls, giveaways, and role panels.
- Other settings configured by server administrators.

Message Information

Some features require access to message content, including prefix commands, autoresponders, sticky messages, and moderation logging.

When server logging is configured, edited or deleted message content may be forwarded to the server's designated logging channel.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed to:

- Provide the Bot's features.
- Maintain server configurations.
- Execute moderation and security functions.
- Maintain leveling and economy systems.
- Deliver reminders and automated responses.
- Support server administration and troubleshooting.

4. Data Storage and Retention

The Bot stores its application data in a local JSON database file on the infrastructure used to operate the Bot.

Stored information may remain available while the Bot continues to operate or until the relevant records are deleted.

Some records may be retained until manually removed or until the underlying server configuration is deleted.

5. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Information is processed through Discord's services when the Bot interacts with Discord. Server logging features may make relevant information visible to authorized server administrators and moderators.

The Bot operator's hosting provider may also process technical information necessary to operate the service.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect stored information and restrict unauthorized access. However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may contact the Bot operator to ask about information associated with their Discord account or request deletion where applicable.

Server administrators may also remove relevant configurations through the Bot's available features or by requesting assistance.

Some records may need to be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws.

9. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's applicable privacy practices and policies.
